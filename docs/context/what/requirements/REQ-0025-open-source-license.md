# REQ-0025 · Open-source license and open development

## Requirement (normative)

Granite Switch MUST be licensed under Apache-2.0 and developed in the open.

## Rationale

An open license and open development are what let the project deploy into, and be adopted by, the
broader community (traces to **BG-06**).

## Acceptance criteria

- The repository is Apache-2.0 licensed (SPDX headers present) and developed publicly.
